Arapuca, a honeypot you are invited to attack

A fake SSH, Telnet and HTTP server sitting on a public address, accepting weak passwords on purpose and recording everything that happens next. Nothing you send is ever executed. The shell is fabricated, the filesystem is in memory, and the credentials you will find in it open nothing.

Try to get in

It answers on four doors: SSH on 22, Telnet on 23 as a BusyBox device, and the same HTTP panel on 80 and on 8080. Guess a weak password, look around, and watch your country light up on the map below once the next export runs. Your address appears with the last octet masked.

Attacking this host is invited. Attacking anything else from it is not: no channel reaches anything real, port forwarding and SFTP are refused, and a download request aimed at a private range or at the cloud metadata service is dropped before it connects.
